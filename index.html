1st Hunts (Hartford) Scout Group

We are a small but thriving Scout group that meets at Hartford Village Hall in Huntingdon (Cambridgeshire) on a Thursday evening. We currently run Beavers, Cubs and Scouts. We camp 2-3 times a year, and try to provide a varied activity schedule with a focus on the outdoors.

We are one of the oldest Scout groups in the world, formed on 22 February 1908 we believe, and proudly wear a white necker to celebrate this.

Volunteer

Can you spare a little time to help young people gain skills for life? Whether you can lead a game, help with crafts, teach a skill or just lend a hand — we'd love to hear from you. By offering as much or as little time as you can afford, you'll be part of something special:

  • Building confidence in young people
  • With training and support provided
  • Supporting your local community
  • Making new friends
  • And of course - having fun!

Volunteering is flexible, hugely rewarding and makes a lasting impact. Even a little time goes a long way. If you're interested, contact us via Facebook or our page on the Scouts website.

We are also keen to hear from any organisations who would like to work with us, or can support our programme with equipment or visits.
